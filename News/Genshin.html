Genshin Impact Version 7.1 Launches September 23 as Forces Gather in Snezhnaya for a Showdown with the Ruler of Death

Product 09/23/2026

Global interactive entertainment brand HoYoverse has announced that Genshin Impact Version 7.1, "A Rekviem for the Underworld," will launch on September 23. With the situation in Snezhnaya becoming increasingly dire, the nation is drawing up a new plan to stand against the Heavenly Principles. Meanwhile, the growing threat of Ronova, the Ruler of Death, is setting the stage for an inevitable showdown. Two new 5-star playable characters, Vesna and Vodyanitsa, will join the roster, bringing new ways to enhance Stellar Swirl reactions. Players will also return to Liyue for the long-awaited Moonchase Festival celebrations.

Check out Genshin Impact Version 7.1 trailer here:

In Version 7.1's Archon Quest, the crisis in Snezhnaya continues to deepen. Players will finally come face-to-face with the Tsaritsa and uncover the true strategy behind her years-long effort to collect the Gnoses. Meanwhile, Ronova, the Ruler of Death, has used her authority to severely hinder Snezhnaya's progress, bringing an inevitable showdown ever closer. The arrival of Rhinedottir, the Ruler of Life, only adds further uncertainty to the unfolding situation. Players will head to the front lines and fight alongside Snezhnaya in a climactic battle against Ronova. The Ruler of Death will also appear as a new Weekly Boss, giving players the chance to confront her overwhelming power firsthand. Completing the new Archon Quest will grant Primogems ×620. Starting in Version 7.1, players can also take on special assignments issued by the Tsaritsa's Confidential Secretary, helping handle matters both great and small while gaining deeper insight into the Tsaritsa's approach to governing Snezhnaya.

Two new 5-star playable characters, Vesna and Vodyanitsa, will bring their own unique ways to enhance Stellar Swirl reactions. The youngest Commander in Druzhna history, Vesna is known for her unwavering loyalty to the Tsaritsa and joins the roster as a 5-star Anemo Sword wielder. In combat, she can convert Cryo Swirl reactions into Stellar Swirl reactions. Using her Elemental Skill and other abilities, Vesna can summon Spirit Blades and gain a certain amount of "Sword Essence." She can then consume "Sword Essence" with a special Elemental Skill to command the Spirit Blades to strike enemies. When they attack near a Stellar Vortex, their attacks are treated as Stellar Swirl reaction DMG. Her Elemental Burst summons another wave of Spirit Blades to deal AoE Anemo DMG while restoring "Sword Essence." Vesna also offers a unique way to explore the open world. As a Vila, she is able to take flight during exploration. Players can also experience a special anecdote featuring Vesna to unlock a unique appearance for her sword spirit companion.

Vodyanitsa is the Prima Donna of Snezhnograd's Korolevskiy Troupe, as well as a kind-hearted Water Imp and 5-star Hydro Catalyst wielder. In combat, she can transform into her Water Imp form and sustain her song through her Elemental Skill, continuously healing the active character, reducing enemies' Hydro and Cryo RES, and boosting the active character's ATK. In this form, she can also move swiftly through the water. Her song further empowers Stellar Vortices, reducing enemies' Anemo RES and increasing Stellar Swirl reaction DMG. Her Elemental Burst carries her voice across the battlefield, dealing AoE Hydro DMG. While exploring the open world, Vodyanitsa can remain in her Water Imp form and move freely even outside the water. Players can learn more about her through a story featuring Vodyanitsa and obtain a unique appearance for her microphone as a reward.

In the first phase of Version 7.1 Event Wishes will feature Vesna and Vodyanitsa, with Skirk and Escoffier returning in the second phase.

The long-awaited Moonchase Festival returns to Liyue after five years, welcoming players back for another celebration! Yilong Wharf has been transformed for the festivities, but beneath the celebrations lies a mystery from the past that even Zhongli and Zibai have yet to unravel. The festival will also introduce three new mini-games inspired by Liyue thaumaturges, with a variety of rewards up for grabs, including a new 4-star Sword.

Back in Snezhnaya, players can join local children in a seasonal snowball event and take on a host of unusual foes. Meanwhile in Natlan, a tournament prediction event will invite players to predict the final results for 16 competing teams over several days of matches. A variety of rewards will be available for completing these events.

Miliastra Wonderland will also see a range of new updates in Version 7.1. The lobby will be refreshed with an autumn-themed look inspired by traditional motifs such as osmanthus blossoms and the moon, and players can unlock an elegant cosmetic set for free by completing the related event. A special festival event will offer even more rewards, including an exclusive headpiece. The editor is also being expanded with new controls that support client-side scripting, along with collaborative editing tools that give players even more ways to create and experiment.

The new version will also mark Genshin Impact's sixth anniversary with players around the world, bringing a generous lineup of anniversary rewards. For the first time, players who complete the latest Archon Quest will be able to choose one event-exclusive 5-star character for free from a select lineup at any point during Version 7.1. Players can also select one standard 5-star character for free, along with Primogems ×1,600, Intertwined Fate ×10, and more anniversary rewards.

Genshin Impact Version 7.1 launches on September 23, bringing players into a pivotal moment for Snezhnaya! With cross-progression and Co-Op functions, players can enjoy their adventure across PlayStation®, Xbox, PC, Android, and iOS. The game has been rated T for Teen by ESRB on PS5, Xbox, PC, and Google Play, and 12+ on iOS. For more information, please visit Genshin Impact's official website (genshin.hoyoverse.com) or follow @GenshinImpact on X, Instagram, and Facebook.
