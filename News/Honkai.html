Flickers of a Spacetime Warp v8.3 Update Announcement

Breaking through countless barriers, she continues her journey just as she did in the past and will in the future.

New S-rank Battlesuit: Introduction of "Planar Armament: Warped Spacetime", a BIO-type Lightning DMG dealer using a Rapid-Shot Crossbow, supported by Astral Ring Specialization: Grail of Infinitude.

Featured Event: "Bon Voyage! Toward Blessings and Wishes!" alongside a unique version-exclusive Life Simulator game mode.

Main Story Part 2 Chapter IX: "If Destiny Concludes Today", unlocking the new Dragon Ruins map area where Helia and Coralie's journey continues.

Equipment & Arsenal: Release of the Rapid-Shot Crossbow "Rainbow From Cinders" (including its PRI-ARM variant) and the "Choices of Fate" stigmata set.

Summer Features: The limited-time "Summer Silhouettes" outfit shop rotation.

Battlesuits & Gears: Fine-tuning and fixes for various characters, including Peregrine Sword, Ba-Dum! Fiery Wishing Star, and Herrscher of Human: Ego.

Game Modes: Updates and stage refreshes for Superstring Dimension, Memorial Arena, and Elysian Realm.

Sitemaps & Systems: Standard structural improvements to the Battle Pass, Bridge interaction features, and Dorm layouts.
